Friedel–Crafts acylation

AlCl₃ turns an acyl chloride into an acylium ion, which the benzene ring attacks. Loss of H⁺ restores the ring. The product ketone deactivates the ring, so only one acyl group adds.

Aromatic substitution · 2 steps

Conditions

RCOCl with AlCl₃, then aqueous workup.

About aromatic substitution reactions

Benzene keeps its stable aromatic ring by substituting rather than adding. An electrophile attacks, then a proton is lost to restore aromaticity.
