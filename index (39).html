Conjugate (Michael) addition

Soft nucleophiles add to the end carbon (β-carbon) of a C=C–C=O system. The enolate formed is then protonated on carbon.

Enolates · 2 steps

Conditions

α,β-Unsaturated carbonyl with a soft nucleophile (CN⁻, enolates, R₂CuLi, thiols, amines).

Mechanism in 2 steps

Red curly arrows show where each pair of electrons moves.

About enolates reactions

A C–H next to a carbonyl is acidic, because the anion formed (an enolate) is stabilised by resonance. Enolates are carbon nucleophiles that make new C–C bonds.
