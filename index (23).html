Wittig reaction

A phosphorus ylide adds to an aldehyde or ketone; a four-membered oxaphosphetane forms and falls apart into an alkene and a very strong P=O bond. Drawn here with Me₃P instead of Ph₃P to keep it small.

Carbonyl addition · 3 steps

Conditions

Ph₃P=CHR (made from a phosphonium salt and a strong base such as BuLi), THF.

Mechanism in 3 steps

Red curly arrows show where each pair of electrons moves.

About carbonyl addition reactions

The carbon of a C=O is δ+, so nucleophiles attack it and the π electrons move onto oxygen. Weak nucleophiles often need acid to activate the carbonyl first.
