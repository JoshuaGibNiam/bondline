Aldol reaction

Base removes an α-hydrogen to give an enolate, which attacks a second carbonyl molecule. Protonation gives a β-hydroxy carbonyl; heating can eliminate water to an enone.

Enolates · 3 steps

Conditions

Dilute NaOH, room temperature (heat for the aldol condensation).

About enolates reactions

A C–H next to a carbonyl is acidic, because the anion formed (an enolate) is stabilised by resonance. Enolates are carbon nucleophiles that make new C–C bonds.
