Questions

Is Bondline really free?
Yes. Notes, drawing, all the practice, sync and classes are free, with no ads. Pro is an optional extra for exam season.
Will free features ever move to Pro?
No. Anything that's free today stays free.
Can I cancel?
Any time, in two clicks: account menu → Bondline Pro → Manage subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for.
What if it's not for me?
Email us within 14 days of paying for a full refund, no questions asked. See the refund policy.
Do I need an account?
For Pro, yes, so it works on all your devices. You can sign in with Google or an email address.
Is there a deal for a whole class or department?
Classes are free for lecturers already. For Pro for a whole course, get in touch.
