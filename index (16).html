Fischer esterification

Acid makes the carboxylic acid carbonyl more electrophilic; the alcohol adds, a proton moves, water leaves and a proton is lost. Every step is reversible.

Acyl substitution · 5 steps

Conditions

Carboxylic acid + alcohol (often in excess) with conc. H₂SO₄, heat; remove water to drive it forward.

About acyl substitution reactions

Carboxylic acid derivatives react by nucleophilic addition followed by elimination: the nucleophile adds to C=O, then the leaving group is expelled and the C=O reforms.
