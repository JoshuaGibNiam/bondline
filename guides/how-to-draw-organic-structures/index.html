How to draw organic structures: skeletal, condensed and 3D

Organic chemists draw molecules as skeletal structures: zig-zag lines where every corner and end is a carbon. Once you can read and draw them quickly, everything else in organic chemistry gets faster.

Structures · Updated 5 November 2026

Four ways to write the same molecule

Take butan-1-ol, a four-carbon alcohol:

FormulaWhat it showsButan-1-ol
MolecularJust the atomsC₄H₁₀O
Structural (condensed)Each carbon and what's on itCH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂OH
DisplayedEvery atom and every bondall C–H, C–C, C–O and O–H bonds drawn
SkeletalCarbon chain as lines, functional groups written outthree zig-zag lines ending in OH

Rules for skeletal structures

  1. Every corner and every line end is a carbon, unless another atom is written there.
  2. Hydrogens on carbon are not drawn. Each carbon has enough hydrogens to make four bonds in total.
  3. Draw chains as zig-zags, with bond angles of about 120°. Straight lines hide where the carbons are.
  4. Write every atom that isn't carbon (O, N, Cl, Br…) and the hydrogens on it: OH, NH₂, CHO.
  5. Double and triple bonds are two or three parallel lines. Draw a C≡C and the atoms on either side of it in a straight line.

Examples

Counting hydrogens

To find the hydrogens on any carbon in a skeletal structure, count its bonds and subtract from four. An end carbon with one line is CH₃. A corner with two lines is CH₂. A branch point with three lines is CH. A carbon with four lines has no H. A double bond counts as two.

Showing 3D shape: wedges and hashes

For a tetrahedral carbon, draw two bonds as plain lines in the plane of the page, a wedge for the bond pointing towards you, and a hashed bond for the one pointing away. You need this for stereocentres and R/S. See R and S configuration.

Common mistakes

  • Five bonds to a carbon. Count the lines at every corner. Four is the maximum.
  • Writing "C" at every corner. In a skeletal structure, carbons are corners, not letters.
  • Forgetting the H on heteroatoms. An alcohol is OH, not just O.
  • Drawing straight chains, which hide the number of carbons.

Quick answers

What is a skeletal formula?

A skeletal formula shows the carbon skeleton as zig-zag lines. Every corner and line end is a carbon, hydrogens on carbon are left out, and every other atom is written in.

How do you count carbons in a skeletal structure?

Count every corner and every end of a line. Each one is a carbon unless another atom is written there.

What do wedges and dashes mean?

A solid wedge is a bond pointing towards you, out of the page. A hashed (dashed) wedge is a bond pointing away from you, behind the page.
