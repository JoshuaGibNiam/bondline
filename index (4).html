Questions

Do I need a Bondline account?
No. Pro goes to the student's account. You only need a card and an email address for the receipt.
Will I be charged again?
No. Both passes are single payments. When a pass ends, the account goes back to the free plan and keeps all its notes.
What if it isn't used?
Email bondlinenotes@gmail.com within 14 days of paying for a full refund. See the refund policy.
Is it safe?
Payment is taken by Stripe. Bondline never sees your card details.
I'm a teacher or run a tuition centre.
A centre licence covers the students in your classes. See pricing for teachers.
