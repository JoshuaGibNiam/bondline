Bromination of an alkene

Br₂ adds across the C=C through a cyclic bromonium ion, so the two bromines end up anti (on opposite faces).

Addition to alkenes · 2 steps

Conditions

Br₂ in CH₂Cl₂ (in water, a bromohydrin forms instead). Decolourises bromine water: a test for C=C.

Mechanism in 2 steps

Red curly arrows show where each pair of electrons moves.

About addition to alkenes reactions

The π electrons of a C=C are exposed and electron-rich, so alkenes react with electrophiles. The two new groups add across the double bond.
