Aldol condensation (E1cB)

On heating, the aldol product loses water: base forms the enolate, which then pushes out hydroxide (E1cB). The product is a conjugated enone or enal.

Enolates · 2 steps

Conditions

NaOH, heat.

About enolates reactions

A C–H next to a carbonyl is acidic, because the anion formed (an enolate) is stabilised by resonance. Enolates are carbon nucleophiles that make new C–C bonds.
