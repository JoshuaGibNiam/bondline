Using Bondline

You can use Bondline without an account. Your notes then stay in your browser on that device, and they're lost if you clear your browser data. If you create an account, keep your password safe; you're responsible for what happens under your account.

You must be 13 or older to create a Bondline account; Bondline asks for your date of birth before you sign in. Anyone can use Bondline without an account. If you are under the age at which you can agree to these terms yourself where you live, please ask a parent or guardian to read them with you and to agree for you before you create an account or buy Pro. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before you buy Pro.

Anyone with a share link can open that shared copy, so only share links with people you trust. You can delete a note, a share link or your whole account at any time.

If you run a class, you're responsible for using your students' names, email addresses and scores only for teaching, in line with your school's or university's rules. Students only share their scores on work set in a class they join, never their notes.

Bondline Pro

Bondline Pro is an optional paid plan with extra study features, currently mock exams, weak-spot analytics, weak-spot practice and exam history. The features in Pro may grow or change over time. We won't move features that are free today behind Pro.

Plans

  • Monthly: a subscription that renews each month until you cancel.
  • Semester pass: one payment for a fixed period, shown at checkout (normally 150 days). It doesn't renew, so you're never charged again unless you buy another pass. Buying a second pass adds to the time you have left.

Payments

Pro is sold through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as our reseller and merchant of record. That means Lemon Squeezy takes the payment, adds any sales tax or VAT that applies where you live, and sends your receipt. Your purchase is also covered by Lemon Squeezy's buyer terms. We never see or store your card details.

Prices are shown before you pay. If we change the price of the monthly plan, we'll tell you at least 30 days before the change affects you, and you can cancel before then.

Cancelling

You can cancel the monthly plan at any time from Manage subscription in Bondline (account menu → Bondline Pro), or from the link in your receipt email. Pro stays on until the end of the month you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Preview period

While Pro is in preview, its features may be free for everyone. We'll announce in the app before that ends.

Refunds

If Pro isn't right for you, email bondlinenotes@gmail.com within 14 days of your first payment (for the monthly plan) or of buying a semester pass, and we'll give you a full refund. No reason needed. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Lemon Squeezy and can take 5–10 working days to appear.

After 14 days, payments aren't normally refunded, but you can cancel at any time to stop future charges. If something went wrong, such as a double charge or Pro not switching on, contact us and we'll put it right. Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under consumer law where you live.

Chemistry content

We work hard to make Bondline's lessons, questions, spectra, predictions and mechanisms accurate, but mistakes happen, and some tools (such as the reaction predictor and predicted spectra) give approximate results by design. Bondline is a study aid: check anything important against your textbook, lecture notes or teacher, and follow your course's conventions in exams.

Bondline is not laboratory or safety guidance. Never use it to decide how to handle, mix or dispose of chemicals. Follow your lab's safety rules and supervisors.

If you spot an error, please tell us at bondlinenotes@gmail.com.
