Epoxide ring opening (base)

Under basic conditions a nucleophile opens the strained three-membered ring by SN2 at the less hindered carbon.

Substitution · 2 steps

Conditions

NaOMe in MeOH (or other nucleophiles: OH⁻, RNH₂, RMgBr). Under acid, attack moves to the more substituted carbon.

About substitution reactions

In a substitution, a nucleophile replaces a leaving group on a saturated carbon. Whether it happens in one step (SN2) or through a carbocation (SN1) depends on the substrate, the nucleophile and the solvent.
