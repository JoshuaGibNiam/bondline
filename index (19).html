Imine formation

A primary amine adds to an aldehyde or ketone, then water is lost to give C=N. Fastest at about pH 5: enough acid to protonate the OH, but not so much that the amine is all protonated.

Carbonyl addition · 5 steps

Conditions

RNH₂ with a trace of acid, removing water. With secondary amines the same route gives an enamine.

About carbonyl addition reactions

The carbon of a C=O is δ+, so nucleophiles attack it and the π electrons move onto oxygen. Weak nucleophiles often need acid to activate the carbonyl first.
