Bromination of phenol

The oxygen lone pair pushes electron density into the ring, so phenol attacks bromine without a halogen carrier. Shown at the 4-position; in bromine water it happens at 2, 4 and 6.

Aromatic substitution · 2 steps

Conditions

Bromine water, room temperature, no catalyst. Gives 2,4,6-tribromophenol, a white precipitate.

Mechanism in 2 steps

Red curly arrows show where each pair of electrons moves.

About aromatic substitution reactions

Benzene keeps its stable aromatic ring by substituting rather than adding. An electrophile attacks, then a proton is lost to restore aromaticity.
