Where to find real past papers

  • Cambridge 9701 past papers pageOfficialCambridge’s public page for 9701, with the syllabus, specimen papers and a selection of past papers.
  • School Support Hub (for teachers)OfficialRegistered Cambridge schools get past papers, mark schemes and examiner reports for the last five years. Ask your teacher.

Cambridge sets several variants of each paper (for example 9701/12 and 9701/22) for different time zones. They cover the same syllabus at the same difficulty, so any variant is good practice.

How to use past papers well

  • Save the newest papers for the last weeks before the exam, and use older ones for topic practice.
  • Time yourself to the real exam length, with no notes. Pacing is a skill of its own.
  • Mark strictly with the official mark scheme, then redo every question you lost marks on a few days later.
  • Track your topics. Note which topics cost you marks and revise those first.
