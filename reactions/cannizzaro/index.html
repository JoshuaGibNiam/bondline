Cannizzaro reaction

With concentrated alkali, an aldehyde that has no α-hydrogen is both oxidised and reduced: hydroxide adds to one molecule, which then passes a hydride ion to a second.

Carbonyl addition · 3 steps

Conditions

Concentrated NaOH or KOH, for aldehydes with no α-hydrogen (methanal, benzaldehyde).

Mechanism in 2 steps

Red curly arrows show where each pair of electrons moves.

About carbonyl addition reactions

The carbon of a C=O is δ+, so nucleophiles attack it and the π electrons move onto oxygen. Weak nucleophiles often need acid to activate the carbonyl first.
