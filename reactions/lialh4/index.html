Reduction of an ester with LiAlH₄

Hydride adds to the ester, the tetrahedral intermediate expels alkoxide to give an aldehyde, and a second hydride reduces that straight away. Two primary alcohols form after workup.

Acyl substitution · 4 steps

Conditions

LiAlH₄ in dry ether or THF, then H₃O⁺ workup. NaBH₄ is too weak to reduce esters.

About acyl substitution reactions

Carboxylic acid derivatives react by nucleophilic addition followed by elimination: the nucleophile adds to C=O, then the leaving group is expelled and the C=O reforms.
