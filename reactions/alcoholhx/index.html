Alcohol to chloroalkane (SN1)

A tertiary alcohol is protonated, loses water to form a carbocation, and chloride attacks. This is why tertiary alcohols turn Lucas reagent cloudy at once.

Substitution · 3 steps

Conditions

Concentrated HCl at room temperature for tertiary alcohols; secondary and primary alcohols need ZnCl₂ (Lucas reagent) or PCl₅/SOCl₂.

Mechanism in 3 steps

Red curly arrows show where each pair of electrons moves.

About substitution reactions

In a substitution, a nucleophile replaces a leaving group on a saturated carbon. Whether it happens in one step (SN2) or through a carbocation (SN1) depends on the substrate, the nucleophile and the solvent.
