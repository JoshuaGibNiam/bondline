Friedel–Crafts alkylation

A carbocation (made from an alkyl halide and AlCl₃) is attacked by the ring, then H⁺ is lost. Problems: rearrangements and over-alkylation, because the product is more reactive.

Aromatic substitution · 2 steps

Conditions

RCl with AlCl₃. Here 2-chloropropane gives the isopropyl cation.

Mechanism in 2 steps

Red curly arrows show where each pair of electrons moves.

About aromatic substitution reactions

Benzene keeps its stable aromatic ring by substituting rather than adding. An electrophile attacks, then a proton is lost to restore aromaticity.
