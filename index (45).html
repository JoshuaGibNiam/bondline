Acetal formation

A hemiacetal loses water (helped by the neighbouring oxygen) to give an oxonium ion, which a second alcohol traps. Acetals are stable to base, so they protect aldehydes and ketones.

Carbonyl addition · 4 steps

Conditions

Excess alcohol, acid catalyst, water removed (e.g. Dean–Stark). Reversed by aqueous acid.

Mechanism in 4 steps

Red curly arrows show where each pair of electrons moves.

About carbonyl addition reactions

The carbon of a C=O is δ+, so nucleophiles attack it and the π electrons move onto oxygen. Weak nucleophiles often need acid to activate the carbonyl first.
