Epoxide ring opening (acid)

Under acid the epoxide oxygen is protonated first; the nucleophile then attacks the more substituted carbon, which carries more positive charge.

Substitution · 3 steps

Conditions

Dilute acid in an alcohol or water (compare base, which attacks the less hindered carbon).

About substitution reactions

In a substitution, a nucleophile replaces a leaving group on a saturated carbon. Whether it happens in one step (SN2) or through a carbocation (SN1) depends on the substrate, the nucleophile and the solvent.
