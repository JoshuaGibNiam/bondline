Hydrolysis of an acyl chloride

Even water is nucleophilic enough to attack an acyl chloride. Addition, then elimination of chloride, gives the carboxylic acid and HCl (steamy fumes).

Acyl substitution · 3 steps

Conditions

Water, room temperature. Vigorous; acyl chlorides must be kept dry.

About acyl substitution reactions

Carboxylic acid derivatives react by nucleophilic addition followed by elimination: the nucleophile adds to C=O, then the leaving group is expelled and the C=O reforms.
