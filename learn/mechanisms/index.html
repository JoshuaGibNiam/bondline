Chapter 1

SN2: one step, from the back

Scroll to play the reaction of bromoethane with hydroxide.

The nucleophile attacks the carbon from the side opposite the leaving group. The new bond forms while the C–Br bond breaks, in a single step through a crowded transition state.

Both reactants are in the slow step, so the rate is second order. The carbon’s configuration inverts, like an umbrella in the wind.

Chapter 2

SN1: leave first, attack later

Scroll to play 2-bromo-2-methylpropane in water.

Step 1 (slow): the C–Br bond breaks on its own, giving a planar carbocation. Step 2 (fast): the nucleophile adds to either face.

Only the halogenoalkane is in the slow step, so the rate is first order. A stereocentre would give a racemic mixture.
