Diels–Alder reaction

A diene and a dienophile join in one concerted step through a cyclic six-electron transition state, making a cyclohexene and two new C–C σ bonds.

Pericyclic · 1 step

Conditions

Heat. Electron-poor dienophiles (C=C–C=O, C=C–CN) react fastest; the diene must be s-cis.

Mechanism

Red curly arrows show where each pair of electrons moves.

About pericyclic reactions

Pericyclic reactions happen in one step through a cyclic transition state, with electrons moving around a ring. No intermediates form.
