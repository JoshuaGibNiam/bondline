Amide from an acyl chloride

Ammonia adds to the very electrophilic acyl chloride; the tetrahedral intermediate expels chloride, the best leaving group. A second NH₃ takes the proton.

Acyl substitution · 3 steps

Conditions

Two equivalents of NH₃ or amine (or one plus a base such as pyridine), cold.

About acyl substitution reactions

Carboxylic acid derivatives react by nucleophilic addition followed by elimination: the nucleophile adds to C=O, then the leaving group is expelled and the C=O reforms.
