Epoxidation with a peroxyacid

The alkene attacks the weak O–O bond of a peroxyacid in one concerted step, so both C–O bonds form on the same face (stereospecific: cis stays cis). Drawn with peracetic acid; mCPBA works the same way.

Addition to alkenes · 1 step

Conditions

mCPBA in CH₂Cl₂, room temperature.

About addition to alkenes reactions

The π electrons of a C=C are exposed and electron-rich, so alkenes react with electrophiles. The two new groups add across the double bond.
