Dehydration of an alcohol (E1)

Acid turns OH into water, a good leaving group. Water leaves to give a carbocation, which loses a proton from the next carbon to form the alkene.

Elimination · 3 steps

Conditions

Concentrated H₂SO₄ or H₃PO₄ and heat (about 170 °C for ethanol), or the vapour over hot Al₂O₃.

Mechanism in 3 steps

Red curly arrows show where each pair of electrons moves.

About elimination reactions

In an elimination, a proton and a leaving group are removed from neighbouring carbons and a C=C forms. Strong bases and heat favour elimination over substitution.
