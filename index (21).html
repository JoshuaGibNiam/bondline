Ester hydrolysis (saponification)

Hydroxide adds to the ester C=O to form a tetrahedral intermediate, which collapses and expels the alkoxide. The final proton transfer makes the reaction irreversible.

Acyl substitution · 3 steps

Conditions

NaOH(aq), heat, then acidify to obtain the carboxylic acid.

About acyl substitution reactions

Carboxylic acid derivatives react by nucleophilic addition followed by elimination: the nucleophile adds to C=O, then the leaving group is expelled and the C=O reforms.
