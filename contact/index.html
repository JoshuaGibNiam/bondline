Help yourself first

  • Inside the notebook, open the account menu (bottom left on a computer) and choose Take the tour or Guide and shortcuts.
  • If Bondline looks out of date, close every Bondline tab and open it again: it updates itself.
  • To cancel Pro, open the account menu, choose Bondline Pro, then Manage subscription.

Who you're contacting

Bondline is run by an independent developer based in Malaysia. Payments for Bondline Pro are handled by Stripe, so receipts come from them. For anything about your purchase, contact us.
