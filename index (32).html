E2 elimination

A strong base removes a proton next to the leaving group as the C=C forms and the leaving group departs, in one step. The H and leaving group must be anti-periplanar.

Elimination · 1 step

Conditions

Strong, often bulky base (e.g. EtO⁻, t-BuO⁻), heat. Rate = k[RX][base].

Mechanism

Red curly arrows show where each pair of electrons moves.

About elimination reactions

In an elimination, a proton and a leaving group are removed from neighbouring carbons and a C=C forms. Strong bases and heat favour elimination over substitution.
