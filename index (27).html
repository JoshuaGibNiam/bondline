Naming esters, amides and other carboxylic acid derivatives

Carboxylic acid derivatives are all named from the parent acid. Count the carbons in the acid part, then change the ending. Esters are the one that trips people up, because the name is written in two words.

Naming · Updated 5 November 2026

Esters: alkyl alkanoate

An ester is made from an alcohol and a carboxylic acid. Its name has two words: the alkyl group from the alcohol first, then the acid part with -oic acid changed to -oate.

  1. Find the C=O. The chain that includes the C=O carbon is the acid part.
  2. The group on the other side of the single-bonded O is the alcohol part. Name it as an alkyl group.
  3. Write: alkyl + space + acid name ending in -oate.

Reading the name back: in propyl ethanoate, the propyl is on the O and the ethanoate (two carbons, including C=O) is the acid part. Its isomer ethyl propanoate is a different compound.

Amides: -amide

Replace -oic acid with -amide. Groups on the nitrogen get N- as their locant.

Acyl chlorides: -oyl chloride

Replace -oic acid with -oyl chloride: ethanoyl chloride, propanoyl chloride, benzoyl chloride.

Acid anhydrides: -oic anhydride

Two acid units joined through an O. If both halves are the same, replace "acid" with anhydride: ethanoic anhydride.

Nitriles: -nitrile

Count the carbon of the C≡N as part of the chain, and add -nitrile to the alkane name: CH₃CN is ethanenitrile, CH₃CH₂CN is propanenitrile.

Common mistakes

  • Swapping the two halves of an ester name. The alkyl group on the O always comes first.
  • Not counting the C=O carbon (or the C≡N carbon) as part of the chain.
  • Writing "methylethanamide" when the methyl is on N. It's N-methylethanamide.

Quick answers

How do you name an ester?

Name the alkyl group from the alcohol first, then the acid part with -oic acid changed to -oate. CH₃COOCH₂CH₃ is ethyl ethanoate.

What is the difference between ethyl propanoate and propyl ethanoate?

They are isomers. Ethyl propanoate is CH₃CH₂COOCH₂CH₃ (propanoic acid with an ethyl group on O). Propyl ethanoate is CH₃COOCH₂CH₂CH₃ (ethanoic acid with a propyl group on O).

What does N- mean in an amide name?

N- shows that a group is attached to the nitrogen, not to a carbon. N-methylethanamide has a methyl group on the nitrogen of ethanamide.
